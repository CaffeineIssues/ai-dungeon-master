# 🧙‍♂️ AI Dungeon Master  
### A Solo D&D 5e Experience Powered by AI

**AI Dungeon Master** is an AI-powered Dungeon Master created for people who **don’t have the time or a group** to play traditional Dungeons & Dragons, but still want a **real D&D 5e role-playing experience**.

Play solo, at your own pace, whenever you want.  
No scheduling. No group. Just you, your character, and the adventure.

---

## 🎯 Who This Is For

- People who don’t have time for regular D&D sessions
- Solo players who want a full role-playing experience
- New players who want to learn D&D naturally
- Writers and worldbuilders looking for inspiration
- Anyone curious about AI-driven storytelling

---

## ✨ Features

- AI acts as a **Dungeon Master**
- Based on **Dungeons & Dragons 5e rules**
- Solo, story-driven gameplay
- Dynamic narration and world reactions
- Procedural quests, NPCs, and locations
- Combat and dice roll handling
- Persistent character and campaign memory
- Play for minutes or hours — your pace, your rules

---

## 🧠 How It Works

You describe what your character does.

The AI Dungeon Master:
- Interprets your actions
- Applies D&D 5e rules
- Describes outcomes and consequences
- Advances the story naturally
- Remembers past events and choices

The game is played through **conversation**, just like tabletop D&D — but powered by AI.

---

## 🎮 Example Session

> **You:** I cautiously open the dungeon door and listen for movement.  
> **AI DM:** The rusted hinges creak softly as the door opens. Beyond it, you hear slow, uneven footsteps echoing in the dark corridor…
